About

Sonata Music Studio is a sanctuary for structured musical growth rooted in cultural richness and reverence. Led by Felix Satia—music instructor, arranger, and composer—the studio combines classical precision with contemplative artistry, inspired by heritage, sacred tradition, and cross-cultural refinement.

Programs

Tailored lessons for different aims — from ABRSM preparation to liturgical ensemble work. Choose a path that suits your ambition.

Piano — Classical Repertoire

Technique, interpretation, and program building for recitals and exams.

Music Theory — Rigorous & Practical

Harmony, counterpoint, analysis — for exam success and compositional thinking.

Choir & Ensemble Coaching

Liturgical choir coaching, ensemble balance, and rehearsal leadership.
