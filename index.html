Welcome to Sonata Music Studio

Sonata Music Studio is a sanctuary for structured musical growth rooted in cultural richness and reverence. Led by Felix Satia—music instructor, arranger, and composer—the studio combines classical precision with contemplative artistry.

Instruction: Piano technique, repertoire, and tailored ABRSM-aware theory coaching. Ensembles: Choral and liturgical coaching for ensembles and choir.
