The Director

Sonata Music Studio is a sanctuary for structured musical growth. Led by Felix Satia—instructor, arranger, and composer—the studio bridges the gap between classical precision and contemplative artistry.

Our curriculum is designed for those who seek more than just technical skill, but a deeper understanding of music as a cultural and intellectual heritage.

Core Programs

  • Piano Performance
    From Baroque masterpieces to 20th-century avant-garde.
  • Theory & Composition
    Advanced harmonic analysis and liturgical arrangement.
  • Sacred Music
    Specialized coaching for choral leadership and liturgical service.

Our Philosophy

We believe in Sacred Discipline. Music is an intellectual pursuit that requires patience, reverence, and a pursuit of meaning beyond the notes.

Blending Western classical rigor with Asian reflective values, we foster an environment where art truly meets intellect.
